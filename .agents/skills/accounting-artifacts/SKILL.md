# Accounting Artifacts

Read `../../../rules/04-accounting-rules.md` plus the relevant DOCX or XLSX rules.

Require explicit entity, period, currency, source, and accounting basis where relevant.

Keep separate:
- accounting treatment,
- cash-flow effect,
- legal ownership,
- tax treatment,
- management reporting,
- assumptions.

For reconciliations, show source/opening balance, reconciling items, target/closing balance, explanation, support, and residual difference.

For journal schedules, distinguish posted, approved-but-unposted, proposed, reversed, and retired items.

Do not use accounting entries as proof of legal execution or tax compliance.

Do not plug unexplained differences. Surface them as findings or blockers.
